Privacy Policy

Covers: World Radio Shuffle · Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use World Radio Shuffle ("the app"). It's built and maintained by an independent developer, not a company. If I publish other apps in the future, each one will be linked from this policy or have its own.

The short version

I don't run any servers that collect your personal data, and there's no account or sign-up. The app plays radio streams from third-party stations, keeps your favorites on your own device, and shows ads through Google AdMob. The rest of this page spells out exactly what that involves.

Information I collect

I don't collect, store, or have access to any personal information myself. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-up. The app doesn't ask for your name, email, or any identifying information.
  • Favorites stay on your device. Stations you star are saved in a local database on your phone. I never see this list, and it isn't uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.
  • No analytics or crash reporting. The app does not currently use any analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDK of its own.

Streaming radio stations

When you play a station, the app connects directly to that station's own streaming server to fetch the audio. This is the same as tuning in to that station from any browser or app: the station's server can see your IP address and basic request information, the same way any website you visit can. I don't control what individual radio stations do with that information — check the station's own site if you want details.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and Google may collect and use information such as your advertising ID, device information, general location (like country or region), and how you interact with ads, in order to show and measure ads.

Google's own policies explain this in more detail:

  • How Google uses information from partner sites and apps
  • AdMob's data collection and use

Depending on where you're located (for example the EEA, UK, or California), you may be shown a consent prompt about ad personalization before ads load, in line with local requirements.

Note for reviewers: this app does not target children and is not listed on Google Play as directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Permissions the app uses

  • Internet — to stream radio stations and load station art/metadata.
  • Notifications — to show playback controls in the notification shade and lock screen.
  • Foreground service (media playback) — to keep audio playing while the app is in the background.

None of these permissions are used to collect personal data; they exist to make playback work properly on Android.

Data deletion

Since favorites are stored only on your device, you can remove them at any time by un-starring a station in the app, clearing the app's storage in Android's settings, or uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

World Radio Shuffle is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and update the date at the top.
