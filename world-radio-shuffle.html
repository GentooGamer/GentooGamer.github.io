World Radio Shuffle

Shuffle into live radio from anywhere in the world

Coming soon on Google Play

What it does

World Radio Shuffle turns discovering live radio into something closer to spinning a globe than scrolling a list. Pick a pin on the map, or just shuffle, and you're listening to a real station broadcasting from that part of the world.

Explore the globe

Browse stations by location, pinned on a world map, rather than an alphabetical list with no sense of place.

Shuffle to discover

One tap jumps straight to a new live station, anywhere on Earth, when you'd rather be surprised than search.

Keeps playing in the background

Playback continues while you use other apps, with full controls from the lock screen and notification shade.

Favorites, saved on your device

Star the stations you want back. They're stored locally on your phone, never uploaded anywhere.

Permissions & data

Here's exactly what the app asks for on your device, and why:

Internet access Needed to stream radio stations and load their station art and metadata.
Notifications Shows the now-playing controls in your notification shade and on the lock screen.
Background playback Uses Android's media playback service so audio keeps streaming when you switch apps or lock your phone.

The app also shows ads through Google AdMob. For the full picture of what that means, see the privacy policy.
