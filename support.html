Common questions

A station won't load or keeps buffering

Check your internet connection first. Some stations' own streams occasionally go down on their end. Try shuffling to a different one, or come back later.

My favorites disappeared

Favorites are saved locally on your device only. Uninstalling the app, clearing its storage, or switching phones will clear this list, since it's never backed up to a server.

Playback stops when I lock my phone

Make sure notifications are allowed for the app and that your device's battery optimization isn't set to aggressively restrict it in the background.
